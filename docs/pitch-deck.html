Precedent

The harness that lets company agents act, learn and be stopped, safely.

Built on MongoDB Atlas · Voyage AI · Claude · Raindrop

Speaker notes: Company AI agents fail in two ways: they do things they shouldn't, and they forget what people taught them. Precedent is the harness that fixes both.

The problem

Company agents fail in two ways.

They do what they shouldn't

Install a web-search MCP that reads prompt-injected pages. Query a finance-only table.

They forget what they learned

Every agent re-guesses the same undocumented warehouse table, every time.

Speaker notes: Precedent sits between the agent and every tool. The model only proposes; a deterministic policy engine on MongoDB Atlas decides, and Raindrop traces everything.

What it does

Block

Web-search MCPs are judged by Claude and blocked by policy before any network contact.

Request

Other MCPs queue for InfoSec, ranked by how many people asked, with Raindrop evidence.

Learn once

A human approves what the agent learned; it is saved in MongoDB, and the next engineer gets it first try.

Improve and stop

It drafts its own guardrails, checked by Raindrop, and quarantines a tool in one click.

Speaker notes: Side by side, the same agent without Precedent connects to anything, forgets everything and keeps using a compromised connector. With Precedent it blocks risky MCPs, queues the rest for InfoSec by demand, learns once with a human and reuses it through MongoDB, writes its own guardrails after Raindrop checks them, and stops in one click.
